Snap Support

Snap is a menu-bar screenshot tool for macOS. It has no Dock icon; click the Snap icon in the menu bar to open the menu.

The first capture shows no image

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, enable Snap, then quit and reopen Snap.

Shortcuts

⌃⌥1 Area · ⌃⌥2 Scrolling capture · ⌃⌥3 GIF · ⌃⌥4 Window · ⌃⌥5 Full screen · ⌃⌥6 OCR · ⌃⌥7 Recognize last · ⌃⌥8 Re-capture last area. You can change them in Settings.

Where files are saved

History lives in the app support folder; choose “Open Folder” from the menu.
