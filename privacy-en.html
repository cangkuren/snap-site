Snap Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-10-28

Snap is a macOS screenshot tool that runs entirely on your Mac. We do not sell, collect or upload your screenshots, text or any personal data.

On-device processing

Captures, scrolling capture, GIF recording and OCR happen on your Mac. History is stored in the app's local container. Content leaves Snap only when you explicitly save, copy or share it.

No data collection

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDK. Screenshots and recognition results are never sent to a server.

System permissions

Screen Recording is used only to capture the area you choose. Snap does not request Accessibility access.
